Spoooky Deck - Credits and licenses

App: Mateusz Staniszewski, https://whosz.pl
Card illustrations, interface icons and backgrounds: AI-generated (Flux 2 Pro via Replicate) and processed by the scripts in tools/.
Music: audio/spoooky-theme-mm.mp3, generated with MiniMax Music 2.6 via Replicate (https://replicate.com/minimax/music-2.6, instrumental version; labelled as AI-generated in the README; the commercial terms for outputs under the MiniMax terms are to be confirmed), processed with a script (loop, loudness). The earlier music from Suno (free plan) was withdrawn.
